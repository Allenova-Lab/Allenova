## Estimating Solar Power Requirements for a Mars City
This project explores the solar infrastructure needed to power a Mars city of 10,000 people. Our calculations show that over 1.5 million solar panels and about 12 km² of land would be required.
[Watch Video Presentation](https://allenova.substack.com/p/estimating-solar-power-requirement)
